# Local images and dynamic screensaver

**Wallpaper → Choose local image** now opens Luma's bundled image picker. No Android document picker or extra app is required. The picker first shows photos already saved in Luma, including an existing imported wallpaper. Use **Allow access to local photos** to show the projector's media-library images, then **Refresh photos** after adding new images to Pictures or Downloads. USB photos must be indexed by the projector or copied into its shared picture folders.

Use Up/Down and OK to select a thumbnail. Shared photos are copied into Luma's private image storage; the selection remains usable after a restart and after the external original is moved. JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported where Android can decode them. Imports are limited to 32 MB and decoded at a bounded resolution. The picker also serves tile-artwork choices. It changes only the profile/field that opened it.

Under **Launcher settings → Screensaver**, **Motion · Dynamic** enables a slow, continuous camera pan and zoom of your selected image. A short eased introduction begins when idle mode starts; the 80-second loop has matching end positions. Image offsets are clamped inside the image edges. Performance mode reduces the animation rate. Choose **Still** to disable motion. Reduced launcher motion defaults this setting to Still unless explicitly enabled.

This animates the camera over a photo. It does not reconstruct a 3D scene, animate objects in the photo, or generate Apple's aerial video footage. The same effect is used by Luma's foreground screensaver and the **Luma wallpaper** Android screensaver provider.

Build validation: release build and release lint passed; 47 JVM tests passed, including shell quoting/foreground guards, bounded image copying and camera-loop continuity. On the P6, the thumbnail picker listed saved and shared copies of the Jonatan Pie photo, and selecting the shared copy imported it and set the wallpaper. Two system-screensaver frames captured five seconds apart differed, confirming the dynamic view was updating. The universal Menu's Keyboard overlay was also exercised end-to-end through its QR/phone Go session in Stremio, producing Matrix search results. Prime Video/Apple TV custom-field compatibility remains unverified.
# Local video wallpapers (1.0.9)

Wallpaper → Choose local image or video lists local images and videos. Selecting a video saves a private copy (maximum 256 MB), extracts a still cover about two seconds into it (one quarter in for very short videos), and associates it with the current profile. Home stays still; both the launcher screensaver and Android dream play the selected clip silently on repeat. Waking stops and releases playback. Unplayable clips retain the still cover. Selecting an image or the included landscape clears the video's screensaver association. Other image pickers (avatars and app artwork) remain image-only.

Video covers use an Android software decoder because the tested P6 vendor thumbnail decoder produced corrupted colours. Common formats require an available software decoder for cover extraction and a working playback decoder. Images retain their existing 32 MB import path.

P6 verification: copied the three supplied aerial MP4s to `/sdcard/Movies/Luma/`, confirmed the picker listed all three, selected Sunrise Coast, verified its corrected later-frame wallpaper, and observed changing frames in the Android screensaver. The player reported looping enabled and zero left/right volume; waking released it. Release build, JVM tests and lint passed. Video files and private device settings are not bundled in source or APK.
